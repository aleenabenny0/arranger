Arranger sets two cookies. Both are needed for signing in. Neither is used for tracking, analytics or advertising, so there is no consent banner: there is nothing optional to consent to.

NameWhat it is forLifetimeReadable by scripts
arranger_sessionKeeps you signed in.Up to daysNo
arranger_csrfProves that a request to change something came from this site and not another one.Same as the sessionYes, by this site only

Your browser may also store the notation engine in its cache. Nothing else is stored on your device.
