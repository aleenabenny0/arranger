What is stored

  • Your account: email address, an optional display name, and a password hash (Argon2id). Your password itself is never stored.
  • Sign-in sessions: a hash of the session token, the network address and browser description it was created from, and when it was last used. You can see and end them on your account page.
  • Your music: the files you upload, the notes read from them, your corrections, and the arrangements, MIDI, MusicXML and PDF files made from them.
  • Your hand profile: the reach, speed, finger and level settings you enter. These are measurements of your hands. They are stored with each arrangement so an old result can be explained later.
  • Job records: when an arrangement, engraving or transcription ran and whether it succeeded.
  • Server logs: the time, path, status and network address of requests, under a request id. Passwords, tokens and reset links are masked before anything is logged.
  • Abuse counters: short-lived request counts per network address and account.

There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking in this software.
