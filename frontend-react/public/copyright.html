Before you upload

Arranging a piece makes a new version of it. If the music is still in copyright, you normally need the rights holder's permission to do that, even for your own use in some countries. Only upload music that:

  • is in the public domain where you live,
  • you wrote yourself, or
  • you have a licence or permission to arrange.

A MIDI or MusicXML file made by someone else can carry its own copyright as an edition, even when the composition is old. Check the terms where you got it.

What happens to your upload

Your files are private to your account. They are not published, shared with other users, or used to train anything.

Reporting a problem

If you believe something stored here infringes your rights, write to with the work concerned, where you saw it, and how to reach you.

The demonstration music

The test corpus used to develop Arranger is public-domain and Creative Commons music from the Mutopia Project. It is not included in this service.
