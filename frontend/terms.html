What the service does

Arranger reads a MIDI file, a MusicXML file or a recording, and produces a two-hand piano arrangement checked against the hand measurements you give it.

What it does not promise

  • “Passes modeled constraints” means every note is within the reach, finger count and movement speed you entered, as this software models them. It is not a promise that you will find the piece comfortable, or that it suits your level.
  • The difficulty figure is an estimate, not a graded-exam level.
  • Audio transcription makes mistakes. Check it before you rely on it.
  • Play within your own physical limits. Stop if anything hurts.
